SolidCraft prototypes

Design prototypes by Daniel Owsianski for SolidCraft, an open-source parametric CAD application. They are personal experiments, not part of SolidCraft, and may change or disappear.

Placement handles: move and turn

A marker that moves a feature across the face it sits on and a ring that turns it.

  • Prototype

    Both handles on a small 3D model, with the drawings as SVG files you can swap in the page.

  • A visual language for placement handles

    The rules the handles are drawn by, how both handles follow from them, the decisions made while trying alternatives, and what is still open.
